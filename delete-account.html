How to Delete Your Account

The fastest way is in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion starts immediately, no email needed.

You can also request deletion via email:

  1. Send an email to support@help-y.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your Helpy account
  3. We will process your request within 7 days
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted
Request Account Deletion

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is anonymized or removed:

  • Profile information - Your name, email, profile photo, phone number, and location are anonymized as part of the deletion process
  • Push notification tokens - Your device push tokens are removed
  • Your posts - Deactivated and no longer shown to anyone
  • Sessions and sign-in identities - Removed, so the account can no longer be accessed

Data Retention

The deletion process begins without undue delay, and remaining personal data is deleted or anonymized within 90 days. A limited set of records is kept where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and abuse: chat messages you sent remain visible to the other participant (shown as "Deleted User"), and proof of your acceptance of our Terms is retained. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Questions?

If you have questions, contact us at support@help-y.com.
