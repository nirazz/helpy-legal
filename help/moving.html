Helpy / Ways neighbors help

An extra pair of hands for the heavy lifting

A sofa that has to go up three floors with no lift. Wardrobes that need to come down to storage. A small flat moving two streets over. Nobody books movers for ten minutes of carrying. On Helpy you ask the people closest to you, and whoever has the time and the muscle offers to help.

For example

  • Getting a sofa or fridge up the stairs
  • Carrying boxes down to storage or a car
  • An extra pair of hands on moving day
  • Moving a table or mattress to the next street

How it works

  1. Get the appHelpy is free on iPhone and Android. Sign in with Google or Apple.
  2. Post or browseSay what you need, or scroll to see who nearby is offering help.
  3. Sort it out in chatAgree on where and when, meet up, then rate each other.

Good to know

  • Say how many items, which floor, and whether there's a lift.
  • Pianos, safes and anything really heavy: book professional movers.
  • Agree on a time in the chat, and let someone close to you know.

Questions

What if nobody answers?

Share the post from the app to your neighborhood WhatsApp or Facebook group so more people see it.

Can this replace a moving company?

Not for a whole flat. Neighbors help with short carries and single items.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app is free, and help between neighbors is never paid.

Who can see where I live?

Posts show a rough area and a distance, never an address. Your exact location is only shared with the neighbor you approve.

Is it safe?

Anyone who reaches out, or accepts someone who did, verifies a phone number first. Profiles carry ratings and reviews, and you can report or block anyone in one tap. We don't run background checks, so whether to meet is always your call.
