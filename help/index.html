What neighbors help each other with

On Helpy you ask the people who live nearby for a hand, or offer one. These are the things people ask for and offer most.

  • HandymanA shelf to hang, a wardrobe to assemble, a dripping tap. Neighbors with tools offer a hand with small home jobs, free, on Helpy.
  • Elderly careGroceries, a weekly visit, help with a phone or getting to an appointment. Neighbors offer free help to older people close by, on Helpy.
  • Tech helpA new phone to set up, a printer that won't connect, an app to install. Tech-savvy neighbors help for free, on Helpy.
  • GiveawayA wardrobe, a stroller, books or kitchenware. Give things away free to people nearby, or ask for something you need, on Helpy.
  • BorrowA drill, a ladder, a tent or a suitcase. Borrow from people nearby and give it back after, free, on Helpy.
  • TutoringMaths, English, exam prep or Excel. Neighbors offer to tutor in what they know, as volunteers, on Helpy.
  • MovingA sofa up three flights, boxes down to storage, an extra pair of hands on moving day. Neighbors help with the heavy lifting, free, on Helpy.
  • RidesTo the train in the morning, to a hospital appointment, or to move something bulky. Neighbors already heading there offer a seat, on Helpy.
  • PetsA midday dog walk, feeding the cat over the weekend, a lift to the vet. Animal-loving neighbors offer help, free, on Helpy.
