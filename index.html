Browse

The right kind of scrolling.

Swipe through what your neighbors need - and what they're offering. Category, distance, urgency, photos, all in one card. Tap to offer help.

  • Requests and help offers, one feed
  • Offer help in one tap
  • Loads what's near you, fast
  • Hides anyone you've blocked or reported
Map view

See your neighborhood.

Switch to the map to see what's needed where. Every post shows as an approximate area - never an exact address. Tap one, it opens right below.

  • Approximate areas, never exact addresses
  • Map and card stay in sync
  • Toggle back to cards anytime
Post

Ask for help in seconds.

Pick a category, write a sentence, snap a photo. That's the whole thing. Your post lands in front of nearby neighbors right away.

  • Post a request, or an offer to help
  • 12 categories, from groceries to repairs
  • Up to 3 photos from camera or gallery
  • No photo? Pick a colorful card look
  • Mark how urgent it is
Chat

Coordinate in real time.

Once you match, chat to figure out the where, when, and how. On a help offer, the chat opens the moment you reach out - no waiting for approval.

  • Typing indicators
  • Read receipts & presence
  • Send photos in chat
  • Mark complete & rate when you're done
Activity

Everything in one place.

One inbox for every conversation, with your posts and their offers right beside it. Accept or decline with a tap, rate when the job's done.

  • Every conversation in one inbox
  • Accept or decline offers in a tap
  • Share a quiet post to your neighborhood group
  • Active and history tabs
Profile

Build a community reputation.

A real public profile with your rating, reviews, and the help you've given. Streaks and a neighborhood leaderboard make showing up feel good.

  • Helped, got help, rating, posted
  • Daily streak counter
  • Neighborhood helper leaderboard
  • A gold badge for neighbors who offer help
Hebrew & English

One app, two languages.

Full Hebrew with right-to-left layout, full English. One tap to switch, the app remembers. Light or dark mode - your choice.

Trust & safety

Safety tools, built in.

Helpy gives you tools - phone verification, reporting, blocking, content moderation. It does not vet users or run background checks; meeting a neighbor is always your call.

Phone verification

Reaching out to a neighbor, or accepting someone who did, takes a verified phone number.

Report & block

One tap to remove anyone from your view. Blocking is instant and goes both ways.

Private by default

Your exact location is shared only with a neighbor you accept. Until then, people see distance, not your address.

Delete anytime

Delete your account right from the app, anytime. Deletion starts right away.
